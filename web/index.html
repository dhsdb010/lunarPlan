    How to land

    You take over at 500 ft in P66; the computer flies the throttle. Keys follow the SpaceX ISS sim. Rotate (the LM's RHC): ↑/↓ pitch, ←/→ yaw, , . roll. Tilting aims the descent engine, which is how you steer toward the green ring and kill drift. Translate: A/D and Q/E fire the small RCS jets for gentle trims (~0.1 m/s²); W/S is up/down, which on the LM is the rate-of-descent switch, 1 ft/s per tap. The RCS jets are weak on purpose (about 0.4 ft/s gained per second held; watch the cyan arrow on the cross-pointer), so use tilt for big drift changes, like the Apollo crews did.

    N opens the nadir panel: a camera under the LM looking straight down, next to a depth map that Depth Anything V2 (small) estimates from that image in your browser. The first open downloads the model (~25–50 MB).

    Pass the 10-7-4 envelope (≤10 ft/s sink, ≤4 ft/s drift), tilt ≤6°, slope ≤12°. EAGLE: ≤30 m from the ring, ≤2 ft/s drift, ≤3° tilt, ≤3 ft/s at the contact light, and ENGINE STOP within 1 s of it.

    Rank: tier, then seconds of hover fuel left. Every score is re-flown by the verifier from your input log.
